Angoliuk is a documentation platform built for teams and individuals who want to publish clear, well-structured content without the overhead of complex tooling. Whether you're documenting an API, an internal process, or a product, Angoliuk's clean, minimal approach keeps the focus on your words — not the interface.

## How It Works

You create a project, add your content using Markdown or the built-in editor, and configure your navigation structure through a simple settings file. Once you're happy with the result, you publish with a single click and share the live URL with your audience. Angoliuk handles hosting, rendering, and updates automatically so you can keep your focus on great documentation.
