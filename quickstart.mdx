You'll be up and running with Angoliuk in just a few minutes. By the end of this guide, you'll have created an account, set up your first project, written your first page, and published your documentation to a live URL.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have the following:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- An Angoliuk account, or a local environment set up for self-hosted use
- Basic familiarity with Markdown

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at the Angoliuk platform and fill in your details to create your account. Once you verify your email address, you'll be taken straight to your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Project">
    Create a new project from the dashboard by clicking **New Project**. Give it a name and an optional description, then confirm to generate your project workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Content">
    Write your first page using Markdown or switch to the built-in visual editor if you prefer a no-code experience. Add a title, some body content, and save your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish** to make your documentation live. Copy the generated URL and share it with your team or users — your docs are now publicly accessible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Need help? Check out the Guides section for detailed walkthroughs of every feature.
</Note>
